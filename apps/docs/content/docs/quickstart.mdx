---
title: Quickstart
description: Create a board, approve a request, and connect your coding agent.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create an account

Open hearloop and click **Sign in**, then **Create account**. Enter your email and a password.

You land on a page that asks you to name your board.

</Step>
<Step>

### Name your board

Type a name, for example `Acme Inc`. The **Address** field fills in with `acme-inc`, and your board is now at `/acme-inc`.

You can type a different address. A few words, such as `login` and `docs`, are reserved.

You land in **Settings**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set up the board

Everything lives under **Settings → Product**:

- **General**: the board name and tagline.
- **Branding**: the accent colour, logo, theme, and the "Powered by hearloop" credit.
- **Access**: who can post, and whether the roadmap and changelog are public.
- **Boards & tags**: groups for your requests, such as *Feature requests* and *Bugs*. See [Requests and statuses](/docs/requests).
- **Statuses**: your own statuses on top of the built-in four.

<Callout>The defaults work. You can skip this step and come back later.</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Share the board

Send people the link to `/acme-inc`. Anyone can read and vote. Who can post depends on your **Access** setting.

</Step>
<Step>

### Approve a request

Open your dashboard. In **Posts**, change a request's status from **Open** to **Planned**. Then open it on the **Roadmap** and pick someone under **Assignee**.

<Callout type="warn">Agents only build **Planned** requests. Open requests are unreviewed public input, so no agent can claim them.</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect your agent

Connect Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or another MCP client. Your browser opens once to sign in, and you don't copy any token. The agent then appears on your board and can be assigned work. See [Connect your agent](/docs/mcp).

</Step>
</Steps>

Next, [build your first request](/docs/mcp#build-a-request) from your agent session.
