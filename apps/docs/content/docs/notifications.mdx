---
title: Notifications
description: Post your board's news to Slack or Discord, and pick which events each channel gets.
---

hearloop can post to a Slack or Discord channel whenever something happens on your board. You connect each one with an incoming webhook, then choose the events it receives.

## Connect a channel

<Tabs items={['Slack', 'Discord']}>
<Tab value="Slack">

1. In Slack, create an **incoming webhook** for the channel.
2. Copy its URL. It starts with `https://hooks.slack.com/services/`.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Discord">

1. In the channel settings, open **Integrations → Webhooks** and create a webhook.
2. Copy its URL. It starts with `https://discord.com/api/webhooks/`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Then open **Settings → Notifications**, paste the URL under **Webhook URL**, and click **Connect**. hearloop sends a test message straight away.

<Callout type="warn">A webhook URL is a secret: anyone with it can post to your channel. hearloop only ever shows its last characters.</Callout>

## Choose the events

Under **Send when**, click an event to turn it on or off. A connected channel saves the change at once.

| Event | Sent when |
|---|---|
| **New request** | Someone posts a request |
| **New comment** | Someone comments publicly. Internal notes are never sent |
| **Status change** | A request moves to another status |
| **Kane CLI verdict** | Kane passes or fails a request |

All four are on by default, and at least one stays on.

## Check delivery

The heading of each channel shows the last delivery, for example *Connected · …a1b2c3 · delivered 2m ago*. A red dot means the last message failed, with the reason. Use **Send test** to try again, **Replace** to paste a new URL, or **Disconnect** to stop.
